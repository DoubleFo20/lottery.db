## 2. Statistical Signal Drift Analysis (Last 6 Draws vs Active Model Signals)

We evaluated the historical probability distributions generated by each of the model's extraction components in `ensemble_model/predictor.py`:

### A. Low-Sum Drift & Parity Anomalies
- **Sum Variance:** Historical mean winning sum is $\mu = 27.0, \sigma = 5.2$. Normal historical envelope spans $20 \le \text{sum} \le 36$ (90.2% of draws).
- **Outliers:**
  - 2026-08-16 (`004615`): Sum = **16** ($Z = -2.12\sigma$).
  - 2026-09-01 (`417212`): Sum = **17** ($Z = -1.92\sigma$).
- **Parity Anomaly:**
  - 2026-07-01 (`751495`): 1 Even / 5 Odds (occurs in only 9.3% of 471 historical draws).

### B. Signal Inertia vs Recency
- `positional_freq` (`ensemble_model/predictor.py:108-118`): Prior weight was 0.20. Relying on all 471 draws created strong inertia toward historical base rates, dampening localized ball dynamics.
- `rolling_heat` (`ensemble_model/predictor.py:121-132`): Prior weight was 0.20 with window=50. Digit `6` and `3` exhibited rapid clustering in recent draws (appearing in 4 of the last 6 draws), which was under-weighted relative to base rates.
- `temporal_trend` (`ensemble_model/predictor.py:235-258`): Prior weight was 0.07. The era-specific 5-year trend had insufficient influence during localized variance shifts.

### C. Search Space Narrowness
- The previous beam width was set to 4 (and 3 in CLI / helpers).
- A beam width of 3 tests only $3^6 = 729$ candidate combinations; a beam width of 4 tests $4^6 = 4,096$ paths.
- With candidate prefixes constrained, high-probability clusters crossing multiple positions were prematurely pruned before joint intra-draw conditional transition scoring (`ensemble_model/predictor.py:432-440`).
